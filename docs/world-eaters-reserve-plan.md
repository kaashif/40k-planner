# Scarabs and winged Prince in reserve: PA vs Purge

20 September 2026 · 11th edition · proposed practice plan, not a VOD reconstruction.

[Scrollable plan with all six terrain diagrams](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/matchups/world-eaters/reserve-plan/).

## Original request, verbatim

> here's what i think i need to do - terminators, wdp off board. everything else as far away as possible from him, but hidden behind terrain. rubrics and sorc and exalted disc sorc on one side. magnus, rubric, tzaangor on one side (how should i deploy that?) and spawn near being able to monkey the middle objective, disc guys can screen for now. then t2 i rapid ingress the dp because he need a charge later, and just normal deep strike the terminators and charge. does that make sense and try to plan that for each possible pa vs purge layout

## Recommendation

Yes: test the two-unit reserve start. Keep the Scarabs’ arrival useful even if they fail a charge, preserve a central scoring route for Spawn, and make the two deployed groups support the same fight. Do not treat “as far away as possible” as the objective: shelter the damage units while retaining a response and a way to score Vital Link.

This updates the earlier suggested on-board Scarab baseline with the user’s chosen reserve-heavy variation. Neither is established as optimal by the small VOD sample. The exact WE list includes two Hades-autocannon Forgefiends, so shooting lanes matter alongside Angron and the four Eightbound/Exalted packages.

### Reserves and timing

- Scarabs 385 + Crystal Terminator Sorcerer 130 + winged Prince 205 = **720 points**, 36% of the intended 2,000-point army. This fits the standard 50% / 1,000-point cap. Attach the Terminator Sorcerer to the Scarabs; all models then have Deep Strike.
- Normal Deep Strike requires more than 8 inches from every enemy. A charge target must be within the modified charge roll, so a minimum-distance unmodified arrival charge needs **9+**, not 8+: 10/36 = **27.8%**; with one failed-roll re-roll, 1 − (26/36)² = **47.8%**. Terrain, model fit, shooting casualties and modifiers may change feasibility. Land to shoot; charge only if it improves the resulting position.
- Reserve at least **1CP** for Rapid Ingress, used at the end of WE movement from battle round two. The Prince still faces their shooting and charge phases. His closer Aetherstride is available only in your Movement phase; it cannot create a 6-inch Rapid Ingress.
- **Going second:** Prince ingresses in WE turn two, survives, then moves/shoots/charges in your turn two. Scarabs arrive normally in your turn two.
- **Going first:** Scarabs arrive in your turn two. Prince ingresses in WE turn two and attacks in **your turn three**. If you need his turn-two shooting, choose ordinary arrival instead; optional Aetherstride forbids charging that turn.
- Have a deeper landing fallback. Starting reserves ordinarily die if they have not arrived by the end of round three. A desired forward pocket is not permission to overlap models or ignore enemy screening.
- Keep Crystal for a later reposition if useful; its Command-phase use requires the brick to be unengaged. A speculative charge can give up that option.

### Deployed groups

**Magnus group:** Tzaangors toward the enemy, Magnus in the protected rear pocket, Rubrics and their Sorcerer offset to one side. Keep a base-width exit clear for Magnus. Do not put Rubrics directly between the Tzaangors and Magnus so the attacker has an easy sequence of contacts. Build a compact coherent screen and check the endpoints available to an actual 100mm flying Angron base. Ground screening is not automatic flight protection. Check both Forgefiends from their actual positions, including Magnus’s wings and terrain visibility rules.

**Support group:** R1 with its Sorcerer and the Exalted Disc Sorcerer nearby. The diagrams assume the Disc Sorcerer joins one bow Enlightened unit (D+B1); this is a bodyguard choice, not another free screening unit. Preserve Incandaeum for the decisive shooting turn. Use spear Enlightened and the other listed bow unit as the mobile screens. Both bow entries in the supplied roster are represented; the plan treats the roster as the user’s intended 2,000 points without removing a unit.

**Robots:** home control and rear/landing coverage. **Spawn:** central control/action when the route and control situation permit. Do not deploy every unit so deep that neither can do its assigned job.

### Why the central objective matters

The stored Vital Link card rewards central control at turn end and operation markers accumulated through Maintain Control; round-two onward Command-phase scoring rewards a non-home objective, with an additional reward for central control. Its action starts in your Shooting phase and requires control at turn-end completion. A unit that advanced or fell back cannot start an action; starting it prevents shooting and declaring a charge, and subsequent movement normally prevents completion. Spawn have an 8-inch move and OC 1 per model; they are eligible for this role but do not automatically beat enemy OC.

Purge’s Destroyer’s Wrath rewards kills and controlling more objectives. A forward screen must buy scoring, deny a route or expose an answerable enemy unit. Avoid offering easy kills without one of those benefits.

## Layout plans

Coordinates below use a 60 × 44-inch landscape board, x increasing rightwards and y downwards, with the blue zone assigned to TS. They are deployment starting points, not a complete base-by-base packing or shooting-visibility proof. Other than Magnus, circles on the maps are unit-group anchors. Mirror/swap the plan for the other agreed side.

The two gold arrival markers are **conditional places to inspect later**, not predicted legal turn-two positions. Check more than 8 inches to every enemy, coherency, physical walls, friendly models, line of sight, countercharges and a useful target. The Scarab box is a planning allowance, not a guaranteed eleven-base formation. If your own units still occupy it, clear them during normal movement before arrival.

### Layout A: Long edges · 20″ gap

Magnus starts behind the home ruin, near the rear edge. R2 is offset to its right; Tzaangors cover the forward approach. Keep the left exit of the home ruin clear for Magnus. R1 and the Disc group stage behind the small lower-left ruins, close enough to support the same central fight rather than disappearing into the far-left corner.

A Magnus centre at (43,41) puts his nearest base edge only about 7″ behind the line: beyond Angron’s direct first-turn reach, but not beyond a fully scouting Eightbound unit opposite him. A blanket promise that everything is safe at the back is impossible here. Reposition against the actual Eightbound starts and cover their ground routes. The 100mm base also overlaps the rear edge of the home terrain footprint in this anchor example: it is not a guaranteed wholly-behind-the-footprint position. Confirm the actual wall blocks visibility, or change the pocket rather than assuming the artwork hides him.

Spawn: Spawn stage near the lower central ruin. Use a legal normal move into range of the nearer central objective, then Maintain Control if eligible and able to keep control through the end of your turn. Do not advance and assume the action still works.

Response: If WE rush the right, Magnus and R2 answer with the arriving Scarabs shooting into that same fight. If WE rush left, withdraw the support group and let them cross into your combined shooting; do not strand the Scarabs there solely to attempt a 9″ charge.

### Layout B: Opposite quarters · 18″ minimum gap

Use the home ruin as the pivot: Magnus behind its southern/rear edge, R2 behind it, Tzaangors toward its northwestern approach. R1 and the Disc group use the lower route behind the small ruins. Do not split those groups between the two distant expansion objectives before seeing the enemy commitment.

Distance is diagonal here. Measure from actual enemy bases on both straight portions of the opposing quarter and its curved boundary. The forward Spawn, Enlightened and Tzaangors are deliberately more exposed than Magnus. A screen buys a response; it is not a second damage group.

### Layout C: Short edges · 24″ gap

Magnus sits on the far/right side of the central home ruin with R2 behind its lower-right corner. Tzaangors occupy the enemy-facing approach, while Robots hold the home area. R1 and the Disc group stage behind the lower-right small ruins; the unattached Enlightened cover the upper route.

With a 24″ deployment gap, important nearest base edges more than 4″ behind the x=42 line deny the full 28″ direct scouting-Eightbound reach. Magnus at (54,21) has about 10″ of setback to his nearest edge. That room is useful, but do not fill his route around the home ruin with friendly bases.
