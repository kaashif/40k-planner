**My starting plan: Magnus and the Scarabs on the table, Prince in reserve—provided the terrain lets you hide both main damage units.** Your aim is to destroy the first committed assault package while keeping a strong reply for the next one.

- **Magnus:** deploy behind a ruin toward one flank, with room to move out and shoot. Check Angron’s route and both Forgefiends’ sightlines. Keep Magnus and the Scarabs far enough apart that one assault cannot reach both through charges and follow-up movement; measure this on the actual table.
- **Scarabs + Crystal Sorcerer:** stage in a separate protected pocket, ready to shoot whatever commits into your front line. If you cannot shelter the brick, reserve it and plan a safe turn-two shooting arrival. Don’t rely on a long charge. **Crystal requires the unit to be unengaged in your Command phase—it cannot rescue a tagged brick.** [Current TS update](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_wh40k_faction_pack_thousand_sons-zha93omuus-lqi5vkrcpq.pdf).
- **Winged Prince:** use it as the delayed response. Look for a protected side arrival that gives it a useful next turn. Rapid Ingress is worthwhile only if it survives the remaining shooting and charges; check the Forgefiends as well as the melee units.
- **Cheap units:** use Tzaangors, Spawn and Enlightened to cover landing zones, contest selected space and protect your damage pieces. Don’t feed them forward automatically. Before offering a screen, check where the attacker can finish and whether you can kill it there afterward.
- **Rubrics, Robots and Disc Sorcerer:** keep the Rubric squads in separate staging positions; use their flamers against exposed infantry. Give the Robots a useful firing/holding lane. Preserve the Disc Sorcerer’s once-per-battle extra Doombolt attempt for the decisive exposed threat.
- **Targets:** prioritize whichever unit threatens Magnus or the brick next turn. That could be Angron or a Slaughterbound/Exalted package. Mark the Battle-lust unit distinctly and check its charge rules. Concentrate enough damage to remove a threat rather than leaving several functional assault units wounded. Shoot a Forgefiend when it controls your safe movement or reserve landing. Crack Khârn’s Rhino only with a plan for the passengers.
- **Scoring:** keep a mobile unit available for objectives. Clear Jakhals/Goremongers when that opens a landing zone or changes scoring. Avoid spending your whole turn chasing Angron while their support units control the board.

The useful VOD comparison is Fowler: **[Scarabs deployed against EC](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/vod/fowler-parry/), [reserved until turn two against Daemons](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/vod/fowler-power/).** Both worked in those wins. For your list, I would test the on-board brick first because you have no Rhino protecting the opening army.
