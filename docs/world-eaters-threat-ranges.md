# First-turn threats and deployment examples: Purge vs Priority Assets

Checked 20 September 2026, **11th edition**, for the supplied Berzerker Warband army. [Interactive examples for all three layouts](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/matchups/world-eaters/).

## Can Angron advance and charge?

Not through the rules available in this supplied build. The [official WE v1.2 update](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_wh40k_faction_pack_world_eaters-5cgvc5tjcb-nipfvwkpo5.pdf), legal from 26 August, changes Unbridled Bloodlust to **+1 to charge rolls**. The [Berzerker Warband Apoplectic Frenzy stratagem](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/world-eaters/#Stratagems) grants advance and charge specifically to **Khorne Berzerkers**. [Angron](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/world-eaters/Angron) has a 14-inch move; his aura about ignoring modifiers to Advance/Charge rolls does not grant permission to charge after advancing. Do not import the old army-wide advance-and-charge blessing.

## Measure actual bases, not centres

The [core rules](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_01-06_warhammer40k_new40k_core_rules-was6fbu1ix-hfewhmxyiy.pdf), §§11.02–11.04, require a target to be within **12 inches** and within the **modified charge roll** when selected. Consequently:

- Angron: 14-inch normal move + at most 12-inch target distance = **26-inch direct first-turn reach**.
- [Regular Eightbound](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/world-eaters/Eightbound): full 6-inch Scout + 10-inch normal move + 12-inch target distance = **28 inches from their original deployment position**.
- [Exalted Eightbound](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/world-eaters/Exalted-Eightbound) with Slaughterbound: 10-inch move + 12-inch target distance = **22 inches** without another movement effect. [Slaughterbound](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/world-eaters/Slaughterbound) gains Scouts itself when attached, but the Exalted bodyguards do not. Core §24.31 requires every model to have Scouts; do not give this whole unit a free Scout move.
- Battle-lust improves charge reliability for its unit, not the 12-inch maximum target distance. With the blessing active, that unit has +2 to its roll and a charge re-roll.

A +1 modifier does **not** extend the 12-inch target cap. Nor should you subtract the 2-inch engagement range from the roll required to select the target. Scout moves must finish more than 8 inches horizontally from enemies. These maxima assume the full Scout move is legal and ignore terrain costs, blocked routes and landing restrictions. They concern direct charges; a nearby screen may permit subsequent pile-in/consolidation contact with something else.

## Angron examples, blessing already active

Straight unobstructed path; nearest base-edge distances; no other movement; optional full charge-roll re-roll. Percentages enumerate the 36 possible 2D6 results. Re-roll success is 1 − (failure probability)². These are conditional dice probabilities, not a simulation of the tabletop.

| Initial gap | After 14-inch move | Natural 2D6 required with +1 | Success | With one failed-roll re-roll |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 inches | 6 inches | 5+ | 83.3% | 97.2% |
| 22 inches | 8 inches | 7+ | 58.3% | 82.6% |
| 24 inches | 10 inches | 9+ | 27.8% | 47.8% |
| 26 inches | 12 inches | 11+ | 8.3% | 16.0% |
| 27 inches | 13 inches | Ineligible target | 0% | 0% |

Example: at a 24-inch initial gap, he moves 14, leaving 10. A natural 9 plus the blessing reaches the required 10. Being “ten inches away” is not negligible risk when a charge re-roll is available.

## The three layouts

Use the current planner maps, synchronized to the 26 August terrain revision. Landscape coordinates below match the interactive page; the artwork cards are rotated. The examples assign WE to red and TS to teal, but that is an illustrative side choice, not a requirement that a faction always gets that colour. Mirror/swap when appropriate.

| Layout | Geometry | Distance-only denial of Angron | Distance-only denial of regular scouting Eightbound |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Opposing 12-inch-deep zones across the 44-inch dimension: **20-inch gap** | Nearest important base **more than 6 inches** behind your line; use about 7 inches for Angron alone | **More than 8 inches** behind your line |
| B | Opposite quarters with central 9-inch exclusion arcs: **18-inch minimum gap** | Actual initial enemy-to-target base gap **greater than 26 inches** | Actual gap **greater than 28 inches**; measure diagonally |
| C | Opposing 18-inch-deep zones across the 60-inch dimension: **24-inch gap** | **More than 2 inches** behind your line; about 3 inches for Angron alone | **More than 4 inches** behind your line; aim around 5 inches |

“More than” matters. Exactly 26/28 inches is still within the maximum direct threat. Distances assume the enemy deploys its forward base edge at the closest relevant point of its line and you remain in that starting position until it moves. If you go first and move closer, recompute from your new location.

### Layout A: space is tight

[Example A](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/matchups/world-eaters/#layout-a) · [Planner](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/planner/?layout=purge-the-foe-vs-priority-assets-a).

Stage Magnus behind the lower-right home area, with the brick in a separate rear pocket. The illustrated Magnus centre is (43,41); a 100mm base has its nearest edge roughly 7 inches behind the y=32 line. Angron opposite cannot directly charge him on turn one, but regular Eightbound opposite can potentially reach after Scout. A 27-inch gap becomes 11 inches after their 16 inches of movement: with +1 charge they need a natural 10+, a 16.7% chance or 30.6% with a re-roll.

Do not claim the whole rear is safe. The area more than 8 inches back is only 4 inches deep, barely wider than Magnus's base. Hide/stage according to the actual scouting units, use legal blocking positions, or put the brick in reserve if it cannot get a protected footprint. The unit anchors are suggestions, not a packed eleven-model legality proof.

### Layout B: deploy into the rear diagonal

[Example B](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/matchups/world-eaters/#layout-b) · [Planner](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/planner/?layout=purge-the-foe-vs-priority-assets-b).

Keep the valuable pieces away from the central arc. The suggested Magnus centre is (56,32), with the brick around (48,40). This preserves separate staging pockets. Evaluate distance to the *actual nearest enemy model*, including models on either straight boundary of its quarter. Moving a fixed number of inches toward one edge is not the same as gaining that much separation from a diagonally positioned attacker. In an idealized directly opposed radial lane, an 18-inch gap needs over 8 inches extra separation against Angron, or over 10 against scouting Eightbound; use measured base-to-base distances instead of applying that blindly to the whole curved boundary.

### Layout C: use the deeper deployment zone

[Example C](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/matchups/world-eaters/#layout-c) · [Planner](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/planner/?layout=purge-the-foe-vs-priority-assets-c).

A nearest base edge around 5 inches behind x=42 puts the target around 29 inches from the opposing x=18 line: beyond both direct first-turn threats. The example Magnus centre (48.5,21) has its near edge 4.53 inches back, giving about 28.5 inches separation against an enemy directly opposite. Stage the brick separately toward the lower-right and check its nearest model, not its anchor. Keep the Prince in reserve and use the smaller units to cover useful lanes without creating an easy combat stepping stone to the damage pieces.

## Screening examples

[Three screening lessons](https://kaashif.github.io/40k-planner/tactics/screening/) cover coherent formations, Angron landing pockets and post-fight consolidation. ## Diagram limits

The interactive page shows a selectable Angron or regular-Eightbound threat from a specified example starting position. The dashed envelope extends to the target's nearest base edge and includes the enemy model's radius when drawn from its centre. Magnus uses a 100mm base; other friendly markers are **group anchors**, not full unit footprints. Both bow units in the user's export are retained. The Prince is in reserve.

The envelope is a straight-line upper bound. It does not pathfind through walls or certify legal charges, visibility, coherency, objective control, or complete deployment legality. Compare the full terrain artwork and physical model bases before placing units. Large infantry groups may extend forward of their anchor. Avoid giving the Forgefiends a free firing lane merely to meet a melee-distance target. A ruin that obscures shooting is not automatically a charge barrier, particularly for flying Angron.
