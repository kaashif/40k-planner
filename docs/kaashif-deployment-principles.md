# Kaashif's deployment principles

These are the default principles for deploying Kaashif's Brighton Take and Hold Necron list. Apply them to each mission layout unless the terrain or deployment zone makes one impossible; any exception should be called out in that layout's plan.

## Unit roles and placement

- **Wraiths:** deploy both units as far forward as practical. Toe each unit into a terrain footprint for cover while keeping legal coherency and avoiding walls that obstruct the intended first move.
- **Skorpekh Destroyers:** deploy as far forward as practical and toe the unit into terrain for cover. Keep the unit arranged for a clean move out rather than trapping models behind the ruin.
- **Skorpekh Lord:** deploy attached to and in coherency with the Skorpekh Destroyers.
- **Ammentar (Nekrosor):** deploy forward and close to the Skorpekh unit so he benefits from Lone Operative while supporting their advance.
- **Nightbringer:** hide fully behind a ruin corner. Do not toe into the terrain footprint; prioritize being out of line of sight over gaining cover.
- **Canoptek Reanimator:** hide fully behind a ruin corner, ideally supporting the main durable units. Do not toe into the terrain footprint; prioritize being out of line of sight.
- **Flayed Ones, forward unit:** infiltrate one unit far forward, usually near the centre and behind a wall. Keep the unit off the terrain footprint so it is hidden rather than merely receiving cover.
- **Flayed Ones, second unit:** use the other unit as a rear or side screen, adjusted to the layout and opposing reserves.
- **C'tan Shard of the Void Dragon:** begin in Strategic Reserves/deep strike.

## Plan checks

Before accepting a deployment:

1. Confirm every model is wholly inside the deployment zone except legal Infiltrators and reserves.
2. Confirm both Wraith units and the Skorpekh unit touch terrain for cover.
3. Confirm the Nightbringer, Reanimator, and forward Flayed Ones have no terrain-footprint overlap.
4. Confirm the Nightbringer and Reanimator are actually hidden from likely enemy firing positions, not just placed behind a wall on the diagram.
5. Confirm attached Leaders are in coherency with their units and Ammentar is close enough to the Skorpekhs for the intended Lone Operative protection.
6. Confirm the forward units have unobstructed first moves and legal base clearance around walls.
7. Confirm the screening unit covers a useful reserve-entry or deep-strike lane.

These principles describe the intended starting position, not a fixed first-turn script. Opponent threat ranges, redeploys, and who has first turn can still change the final placement.
