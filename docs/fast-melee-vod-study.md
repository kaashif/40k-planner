## Which armies belong in the search?

“Fast melee” here means a **list that can deliver meaningful combat pressure before TS can comfortably clear the midfield**. Movement, forward deployment, reserves and transports can all create that pressure. The labels below are a scouting checklist, not a statement that every list in a faction plays this way. Check the actual roster and current rules before assigning threat distances.

## What happened, and what to practise

### Blood Angels: retain a response outside the first collision

At [00:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=2400s), TS are on the right and Blood Angels on the left. At [00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=3000s), TS have a forward line beside the centre-right ruin, large models behind it, and a separate northern group. By [01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=5400s), Blood Angels have reached the central ruins and that forward line has thinned. This is **not** footage of a screen keeping every attacker away.

The score reaches **24–8** at [01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=6000s). At [02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=7500s), dark assault models cluster around the right-side threats. The large cream-based monster, a roster-confirmed Defiler, is still on-board then; at [02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=7800s) it is off the board at the right edge. Magnus remains. This brackets a visible removal without identifying the killing unit or rule.

At [02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M7iDLZU9kks&t=8400s), the northern TS group has shifted down, the right-side Blood Angels cluster is smaller, and the displayed lead has narrowed to **24–19**. The late score jumps during finishing/cleanup. Do **not** turn gathered models into a diagram of an endgame charge, or interpret the jump to 90 as one turn's scoring.

**Practice inference:** stage a second damage/scoring group outside the first collision. Before offering a forward unit, identify where the attackers will end up and which surviving unit can answer them. This win survived an expensive model's removal; it does not prove losing that model was necessary or optimal. The recovered list identifies the ten-Scarab brick as the likely northern group; exact model-to-squad assignments, charges, fight order and damage remain unresolved.

### Slaanesh Daemons: use an outside lane while other units keep playing the board

At [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=24000s), Magnus is left-middle and most Daemons are on the right. At [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=25200s), Magnus is behind the far-left ruin while Daemons contest the centre. At [07:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=26700s), TS infantry still occupy separate left-side pockets. The score is **18–16**.

Magnus reaches the upper lane by [08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=28800s), with TS ahead **39–16**. At [08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=30000s), large Daemon models converge near him while another Daemon group presses the lower edge. Smaller TS units still occupy left-side positions. At [08:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=32100s), a TS group is visible at the far right. The partial pregame list includes an Infernal Master with Umbralefic Crystal, but **that does not prove this group's identity or arrival mechanism**.

TS groups occupy centre and right at [09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=34200s). The [09:40 closing match row](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0xPGlVtX_g&t=34800s) reads **99–38 / 20–0**. Its bottom **93–67** is the live eight-board national-team aggregate, not the match score. The Netherlands ultimately lost the team round **71–89** despite this individual win, per the linked round results.

**Practice inference:** keep an escape/attack lane for Magnus and avoid committing every scoring unit to his fight. Check both flanks before using the response unit. The footage supports the change of angle and dispersed TS presence; it does not identify the movement ability, exact monster casualties or why every combat succeeded.

### A usable pregame and turn checklist

1. Identify the opponent's **first wave, follow-up wave and delivery mechanism** from their actual list. Confirm relevant rules and measure real paths on your table.
2. Choose what you can afford to expose. Position your next response so one assault does not automatically reach every important piece; check the actual charge and follow-up movement possibilities.
3. Preserve a lane for Magnus and a separate scoring/damage group. The two new games demonstrate different versions of this rather than one fixed castle formation.
4. Before answering a melee commitment, check what remains hidden behind it and what pressures the other flank. Plan the following turn as well as the immediate kill.
5. Record points separately from casualties. Ask whether a trade buys a scoring turn or removes the next threat, not merely whether it kills an expensive model.

These are hypotheses for practice derived from visible positions. They are not proof of either player's intent. Two wins cannot establish that a tactic causes wins, and they cannot substitute for a matched loss review.

## Reproduction and limitations

Run `scripts/sample-vod.py` with the video's ID and `tracking.reviewedSeconds` from its `game.json`; raw files remain under ignored `.cache/vod/<id>/`. Publish the selected stills with:

```sh
uv run --with pillow python scripts/publish-vod-frames.py --game yarin-iyer
uv run --with pillow python scripts/publish-vod-frames.py --game most-fritschen
node --test scripts/test-vod.mjs
```

Published images preserve the full broadcast composition and credit Shut up and Roll / Targetpriority40k. First coarse sampling overshot the Huxleys video's 9,575-second duration; 9,600/10,800 failed and are excluded from reviewed timestamps. English caption retrieval returned HTTP 429, so neither new review claims transcript-derived actions. No full videos, private cookies, signed media URLs or fabricated frames are committed.

Maps omit hidden/unidentifiable pieces. Dotted arrows link sampled sightings, not exact paths; most generic infantry groups have no persistent tracking key. Terrain is hand-traced, not measured. Both complete TS event lists were recovered, but neither game provides a verified Terminator reserve declaration, so both remain **unknown** in the reserve tally.
