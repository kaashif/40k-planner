## My starting plan for your matchup

**Start Magnus and the Scarab brick on the table if the terrain gives both a protected staging pocket. Reserve the winged Prince as the delayed response.** This is a proposed baseline to test, not a conclusion that on-board Scarabs always beat WE. The two large damage pieces plus the Prince represent 1,175 listed points; beginning with both the brick and Prince off-board leaves a much thinner opening army. Your lack of a Rhino makes that tradeoff more important.

**Magnus:** deploy toward one side behind an obscuring ruin, with a legal exit to a useful firing lane. Choose the pocket after checking Angron's actual paths and both Forgefiends' sightlines. Keep enough room for Magnus's base to move past your own units. Put the Scarabs in an adjacent but independent pocket: one charge and its follow-up movement should not contact both. A screenshot is not a measurement; test the actual bases, terrain and current movement rules.

**Scarabs and Crystal leader:** use the brick as the shooting reply to the first serious commitment. Keep it unengaged if you want the Crystal option. If deployment terrain cannot shelter it, use starting reserves instead and plan a turn-two shooting arrival into a lane you can actually keep open. Eleven models need a real footprint. A speculative enemy-backfield drop through Jakhals/Goremongers screening is not a reliable plan. Do not make a long reserve charge the condition for your damage turn to work.

**Prince:** reserve it when the remaining units can hold the opening. Prefer a protected side arrival from which the next turn offers a shot and a useful movement/charge option. Rapid Ingress is a tool for that sequence, not protection from the rest of the opponent's turn. Check both Forgefiend sightlines and all legal enemy charge paths before choosing the spot. Fowler–Power is a warning: the Prince survives its first relevant exchange on only two wounds. If no safe arrival exists, use the safer legal arrival and accept a less dramatic turn.

**Rubrics:** stage the two led squads separately, so one assault cannot consume both. Their flamers can answer exposed infantry while the brick/Magnus handle harder targets. You do not have Fowler's Rhino: do not copy his passenger protection or disembarkation positions. Keep the Disc Sorcerer in a protected staging position with access to the decisive ritual target. The export does not clearly specify his attachment; settle that in the army declaration rather than inventing one here.

**Robots, Tzaangors, Spawn and Enlightened:** allocate jobs before deployment—home/landing-zone coverage, one contested lane, one mobile scoring option. Avoid sending every cheap unit forward as successive free kills. Offer a unit only when its loss preserves a damage piece, prevents a better enemy landing, or buys scoring that is worth the trade. Check whether the enemy can charge it and use follow-up movement to reach the brick. Robots should have a useful firing/holding position; do not assume they can absorb Angron merely because they look durable.

## Priorities against this exact opponent

1. **Handle the threat that can reach the key unit next turn.** If Angron can reach Magnus or the Scarabs, solve that immediately by movement, blocking a legal landing/path, or a realistic concentrated kill. If Angron is committed elsewhere and a Slaughterbound unit can reach your brick, the latter may be the urgent target. A fixed “always shoot Angron” order ignores the board.
2. **Mark the Battle-lust unit distinctly.** Its charge reliability differs from the other Exalted unit. Check the current rule in the opponent's app before deciding a gap is safe; do not apply one generic Eightbound threat radius to every unit.
3. **Concentrate the response.** Aim to remove one exposed assault package rather than chip all four Eightbound/Exalted packages while leaving each able to charge. Make your next position safe from the other three. This is a tactical recommendation, not a simulated damage guarantee.
4. **Treat the Forgefiends as part of the trap.** They can threaten the infantry you expose to stop melee and constrain the Prince landing. If one controls your only safe route, removing or denying it a sightline can be more valuable than shooting a distant combat unit.
5. **Plan the Rhino and contents together.** Opening it matters only if Khârn and the Berzerkers can then be handled or denied a useful response. Do not spend the whole shooting phase cracking the transport and leave the dangerous contents in an ideal position.
6. **Clear support when it changes scoring or movement.** Jakhals/Goremongers restrict landings and hold space. Use suitable smaller shooting where possible; do not spend the entire Scarab activation on a cheap squad unless that unlocks the game. Measure remaining screening coverage before banking on a future reserve location.

Going first: occupy protected staging positions, take a safe productive shot if available, and retain the reply. Going second: reassess after the first WE move; punish an exposed commitment if you can do so without exposing both damage pieces. In either case, re-evaluate active abilities and actual paths each turn. The plan is to survive the first commitment with enough damage and board presence to win the next exchange, not to make every screen survive.

## Rules snapshot and important correction

The [official TS v1.2 update](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_wh40k_faction_pack_thousand_sons-zha93omuus-lqi5vkrcpq.pdf) and [official WE v1.2 update](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_wh40k_faction_pack_world_eaters-5cgvc5tjcb-nipfvwkpo5.pdf) are legal from **26 August 2026**, after the reviewed games. They supplement the Codex rather than reproduce every datasheet. Current app exports differ in some points from the August event lists.

The current Crystal update requires the unit to be **unengaged in your Command phase**, places it in strategic reserves and requires ingress in your next Movement phase, granting Deep Strike for that interval. It therefore cannot simply extract a tagged brick. Incandaeum permits an additional Doombolt attempt once per battle even after another model attempted that ritual; plan it for the decisive exposed threat rather than assuming it is a repeatable second Doombolt every turn. Source: official TS update, Rules Updates page.

No 10th-edition reserve deadlines, charge bonuses, consolidation distances or overwatch assumptions have been silently imported. Confirm the current mission/force-disposition rules and unit abilities in the app before play. This plan deliberately does not claim exact damage or a universally safe separation in inches.
