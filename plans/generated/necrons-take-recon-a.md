# Take/Recon Layout A – shelf pressure and screened sweep

Army: Ammentar Destroyer Necrons 1995 (1995 points)

Use the blue forward shelf to start one Wraith brick, both C'tan, Ammentar and the Destroyer package close to the centre. The second Wraith brick takes the left natural while both Flayed One units screen the deep back edge against Reconnaissance redeployments. Pressure centre and the far natural without exposing the home objective to a free sweep.
